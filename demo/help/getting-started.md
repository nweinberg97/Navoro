# Getting started with Lumen dashboards

Lumen is an analytics workspace for operations teams. This article walks through the first things most people do on the dashboard. All names and data are fictional.

## Feature: The dashboard

The dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It shows a row of key metrics for your workspace, a trend chart, and a list of recently updated reports. You can reach it any time from **Dashboard** in the sidebar.

## How to change the date range on the dashboard

Prerequisites: You need the Viewer role or higher.

1. Open **Dashboard** from the sidebar.
2. Find the **Date range** menu at the top right of the Dashboard page.
3. Choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, or **Last 90 days**.
4. Review the key metrics. Every metric and sparkline updates to the period you chose.

The date range is remembered only for the current visit. Scheduled reports always use their own period.

## How to create your first dashboard filter

Dashboard filters narrow the key metrics to the part of the business you care about. Lumen creates filters from saved reports, so you need one report first.

Prerequisites: At least one report. See "How to create a report" in the Help Center if you do not have one yet.

1. Open **Reports** from the sidebar.
2. Click **New report** in the top right of the Reports page.
3. Enter a **Report name** such as "Orders by region".
4. Choose a **Data source**, then pick a **Chart type**.
5. Click **Create report**. The report appears at the top of the reports list.
6. Return to **Dashboard** from the sidebar and use the **Recent reports** list to open it.

## Troubleshooting: The metrics look empty

If every metric shows zero, check that the **Date range** is not set to a period before your data was connected, then confirm a data source is connected under **Integrations**.

## Glossary

- **Date range** — The period used for the key metrics on the dashboard.
- **Key metrics** — The four summary cards at the top of the dashboard.
- **Recent reports** — The reports in your workspace that changed most recently.
